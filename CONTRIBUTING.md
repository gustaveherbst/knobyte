# Contributing to Knobyte

Thank you for contributing to Knobyte.

Knobyte uses a dual-license model. Contributions must preserve the project's ability to distribute Knobyte under AGPL-3.0 and under separate commercial licenses.

## Contributor requirements

By submitting a contribution, you represent that:

1. You have the legal right to submit it.
2. It is your original work or you have sufficient rights to contribute it.
3. You are not knowingly introducing material incompatible with Knobyte's licensing model.
4. If an employer or another party may own rights in your work, you have obtained the required permission.

## Contributor License Agreement

Substantial external contributions should be accepted only after the contributor accepts the Knobyte Contributor License Agreement (`CLA.md`).

The CLA is intended to let contributors retain ownership while granting the project sufficient rights to maintain Knobyte's open-source and commercial licensing paths.

## Workflow

- Discuss substantial changes before implementation.
- Keep pull requests focused.
- Include tests for behavioral changes.
- Update documentation for interface or behavior changes.
- Explain significant architectural implications.
- Identify the provenance and license of third-party material.
- Follow repository security and review requirements.

## AI-assisted contributions

AI-assisted development is welcome, but the human contributor remains responsible for correctness, security, provenance, licensing, and review of generated material.

## Security

Do not disclose suspected vulnerabilities through public issues. Follow `SECURITY.md` when present.

## Project license

The open-source distribution is AGPL-3.0. Separate commercial licensing is available from the applicable Knobyte copyright holder or licensing entity.
